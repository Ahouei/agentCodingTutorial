01

One café, built chapter by chapter

Today the hero builds "Café Sahar", the vibe-coding lab builds "Bean & Bit", the brief builder starts from "Nan-e Shirin" bakery, and the deploy labs use my-cafe and my-site. A learner never sees one project grow. Make Sahar's café the protagonist: every lab works on cafe-sahar.ir, and every chapter ends by adding its piece to the site. The finale shows the whole build history.

02

A hero that knows where you are

The coral «قهرمان» measures 2.85:1 on the cream background, under the 3:1 minimum for large text, and "accent one word" is a common AI-design tell. Keep the headline in ink and give the hand-drawn underline a job: it becomes the learner's progress line. Returning learners get a "continue" card with the exact section, not just the chapter.

03

Journey map: "you are here", not a wall of 18 cards

The map shows every chapter at full size, done or not, so a learner on chapter 9 scrolls past eight finished cards. Collapse finished chapters into small ticks, give the current one a big "you are here" card with section progress, and show only the next three in detail.

04

Chapter opening: a mission brief and a warm-up

Each chapter opens with a title, tagline and Bit's line, then goes straight into text. Add a three-part mission brief (what you'll be able to do, time, what the café gains) and one warm-up question from the previous chapter: recalling something is what makes it stick. Also isolate Latin words in Persian titles so the comma stops jumping (today «Domain، DNS» renders out of order).

05

Reading on a phone: tap any term, jump to any section

Under 980px the section rail disappears, so phone readers lose their place and their progress. And a beginner who meets "TTL" again in chapter 15 has to find chapter 3 to recall it. Add a bottom bar with section progress and a sections sheet, and make every glossary term tappable everywhere. Both sheets close with Back, a tap outside, or a downward flick.

07

One anatomy for all 17 labs (and labs for the 3 chapters without one)

08

Motion: fewer, better moments

Today every paragraph fades in with a 6px blur (about 17 per chapter), every small Bit wiggles its antenna forever, and route changes jump instantly. Flip it: text is simply there; headings and labs reveal once; small Bits are still; and the one moment worth animating, opening a chapter, morphs the map icon into the chapter badge. Easing lives in one token file instead of 29 copies.

09

Color: fix the contrast, consider a Persian identity

Two fixes and one option. Faint text (#9a8b7c) is 3.07:1 on paper; #7a6b5c is 4.78:1. Dark-mode stickers use a black offset shadow on a near-black page (1.11:1, invisible), so cards look flat with a harsh light outline; a warm coral-brown shadow and a softer outline fix it. Option: warm cream + terracotta is the most common AI-generated palette. A "Kashi" palette (turquoise firoozeh, saffron, pomegranate, from Persian tilework) gives the course its own identity. Ship it as a second theme to compare live before switching.

10

Finishing: earned "done", and a certificate with your live site on it

"I finished this chapter" is self-declared, so the progress ring measures button presses. Suggest "done" when the quiz was answered and the lab completed (a light bar, not mastery), and end the course with a named certificate showing the learner's own live URL, ready to share. No streaks or loss-framing; "continue at §3" is the nudge.
